About

At WoodCut Pro, we deliver smart, integrated solutions in furniture manufacturing — combining CNC precision, automated edge finishing, concealed assembly systems, and integrated connections — to create sleek, functional designs that serve specialized spaces with style and efficiency

CNC - Computer Numerical Control

This smart system delivers exceptional precision and efficiency, tailored for high-speed production lines. It handles complex tasks like panel cutting, drilling for runners, hinges, handles, adjustable shelves, and concealed connectors such as Mini Fix and wooden dowels — all with unmatched accuracy. It also automates cabinet back panel sorting, turning imagination into tangible, professional-grade products.

Edge Banding Machine

A fully automated edge finishing solution using PVC or ABS tapes, designed to deliver premium-quality results. From precise trimming and rounding to final polishing, the machine transforms edge banding into a refined process — adding lasting durability and aesthetic value to every piece.

Material

Mini Fix

A smart, widely used assembly system in modern furniture manufacturing. It ensures strong, hidden joints for clean aesthetics — ideal for kitchens, cabinets, and office furniture. Perfect for pieces designed to be disassembled and reassembled without damage.

Woden Dowel

An invisible fastening method that ensures clean, solid joints — ideal for assembling cabinets, drawers, and fixed shelves. A reliable choice for modular furniture designed for easy disassembly and reassembly.

Furniture Screw

Used to securely assemble and connect cabinets with precision, allowing safe disassembly and reassembly without compromising the product’s aesthetics or refined finish.

Metal Shelf Studs

At WoodCut Pro, we use high-quality metal shelf studs engineered with precision to deliver reliable, secure support. Their streamlined design enhances both durability and elegance, adding lasting functional and visual value to every shelving unit.
